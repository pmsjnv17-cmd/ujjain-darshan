About Ujjain

Ujjain is one of the seven sacred cities of India and the home of the Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga. It was the capital of the Avanti kingdom and later the court of King Vikramaditya, after whom our university is named.

The city is also known for the Vedh Shala, an old observatory built in the 18th century, and for being a prime meridian of ancient Indian astronomy. Today it blends temple life with a growing university town.

Places to visit

Mahakaleshwar Temple

The most visited temple in the city, known for the early-morning Bhasma Aarti.

Open 4:00 AM to 11:00 PM

Ram Ghat

The main bathing ghat on the Shipra and the heart of the Simhastha Kumbh fair.

Best at sunset

Kal Bhairav Temple

A shrine where devotees offer liquor as prasad, a custom found almost nowhere else.

Open all day

Vedh Shala Observatory

An 18th-century observatory built by Maharaja Jai Singh II with large stone instruments.

Open 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM

Harsiddhi Temple

A Shakti Peeth with tall lamp pillars that are lit in the evening.

Evening visit recommended

Sandipani Ashram

The place where Krishna and Sudama are said to have studied.

Open 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM
